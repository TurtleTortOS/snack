SNACK

Snapdragon NPU AI Chat Kit — a local AI kit for Snapdragon Surfaces.

SNACK runs an open-weight model on the device's NPU (not the CPU or GPU) via Qualcomm's GenieX runtime, and exposes it through a standard OpenAI-compatible endpoint on 127.0.0.1:18181. Chat happens in the bundled Hermes app; SNACK is the control center for starting the server, choosing the model, and setting context.

TargetMicrosoft Surface, Snapdragon X Plus (X1P42100), Windows ARM64, 16 GB
RuntimeGenieX 0.6.1 (BSD-3-Clause)
ModelsQwen3.8-4B / Qwen3.8-9B, Q4_0 GGUF (Apache-2.0)
Endpointhttp://127.0.0.1:18181/v1 (loopback only)
